## 4. Pelliot chinois 2778 (Dunhuang manuscript)
- **Use:** Source of the five torn fragments on the home page (assets/img/fragments/)
- **Date:** between 800 and 1000? (Commons file page)
- **Author:** Unknown author
- **Rights:** Public domain (Commons: PD-old-100, Public Domain Mark); reproduction published by the BnF in Gallica, free for non-commercial reuse with the credit "Source gallica.bnf.fr / BnF"
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pelliot_chinois_2778.jpg
- **Original file:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b2/Pelliot_chinois_2778.jpg
- **Original work:** Bibliothèque nationale de France, Département des Manuscrits, Pelliot chinois 2778: dhāraṇī in Siddham script with a phonetic transcription in Chinese characters; roll of 2 leaves, paper, 30.4 × 40.8 cm (BnF catalogue record https://archivesetmanuscrits.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cc119412j, read 29 October 2026). A manuscript of about 800–1000 by an unknown author is in the public domain.
- **Digital copy:** the BnF catalogue record links two scans in Gallica: https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b8301748c (made from the original) and https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b100963604 (made from microfilm R 10305). The Commons file names the BnF as source and is in the categories "Files from Gallica" and "Images from Gallica to be checked". Which of the two scans it comes from was not confirmed: Gallica showed a security check that only a person can complete.
- **Publisher's reuse conditions (BnF, Gallica):** the "Conditions d'utilisation" notice printed on Gallica documents says that most Gallica contents are reproductions of public-domain works, that their non-commercial reuse is free provided the source credit is kept, that commercial reuse requires a paid licence, and that the conditions are governed by French law (read on 29 October 2026 in a Gallica PDF, because the conditions page itself sat behind the same security check). This project is a non-commercial academic work: compatible, with the credit below. A commercial reuse would need a BnF licence.
- **Commons statement (read 29 October 2026):** PD-old-100 with Public Domain Mark; "free of known restrictions under copyright law". The page notes that a United States public-domain tag should be added; none is shown.
- **Attribution used:** "Pelliot chinois 2778, Bibliothèque nationale de France. Source gallica.bnf.fr / BnF (via Wikimedia Commons), public domain".
- **Our copy and changes:** `manuscript_pelliot_chinois_2778.jpg` has the size (1,133 × 855) and JPEG comment of the Commons original and is visually identical (same shelfmark label and stamp); its checksum differs, so it is a re-saved copy. `final_report_files/site_build/make_fragments.py` cuts five rectangular regions and gives them a generated torn edge; nothing is added. The fragments regenerate pixel for pixel.
- **Open points:** the scan behind the Commons file (original or microfilm) and the missing US tag on Commons; neither changes the non-commercial use here.
